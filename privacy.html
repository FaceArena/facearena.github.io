Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FACE ARENA handles information when you create an account, upload photos, participate in photo battles, vote, and use the platform.

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. About FACE ARENA

FACE ARENA is an online photo competition platform. Users can create accounts, submit photos to the competition queue, participate in head-to-head battles, vote on competing photos, view results, and follow participant rankings and champion statistics.

This policy applies to the FACE ARENA website at https://facearena.github.io/ and its related platform features.

2. Information processed

Account and sign-in information. When you register or sign in, the platform processes your account identifier and email address. If you use Google Sign-In, Google provides authentication information to the authentication service. For email registration, authentication credentials are handled by the authentication provider; FACE ARENA does not store passwords as readable text.

Profile information. The platform may process your display name, username, avatar file path, profile verification state, and profile visibility preferences.

Photos and competition activity. The platform processes uploaded photo files and related records, including photo ownership, queue or competition status, battle participation, votes, vote comments, results, and timestamps.

Settings and notifications. The platform stores settings such as profile visibility, history visibility, security notifications, and battle notifications. If you enable browser push notifications, the platform processes the push subscription information needed to deliver them, including its endpoint and cryptographic subscription keys.

Technical information. Your browser and the infrastructure providers may process technical request information needed to deliver, secure, and operate the website. This policy does not claim that FACE ARENA builds advertising profiles or sells browsing histories.

3. How information is used

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Maintain participant profiles and display names.
  • Store and display photos submitted to the competition.
  • Organize the queue and head-to-head battles.
  • Record votes and comments and calculate competition results.
  • Maintain battle history, rankings, wins, losses, and champion statistics.
  • Apply profile and history visibility preferences.
  • Send battle and security notifications when enabled.
  • Protect the platform, investigate abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Photo visibility and competition history

Photos submitted to FACE ARENA are used as competition entries. A photo waiting in the queue is not necessarily visible to other participants. Photos may be displayed when they enter an active battle or reach a competition state that is shown in the arena, participant profiles, or results.

Completed battle records and associated photos may remain available in profiles and competition history after a battle ends. Removing an entry from active participation does not automatically mean that its historical result or record is erased.

Do not upload a photo unless you have the rights and permissions needed to submit it and understand that it may be shown to other platform users as part of the competition.

5. Visibility controls

FACE ARENA includes settings for profile visibility and competition-history visibility. These settings are intended to control the corresponding profile and history displays. They do not necessarily remove information already processed for battle operation, integrity, or historical results.

6. Service providers and external services

FACE ARENA relies on third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, and file-storage infrastructure. Account, profile, photo, competition, and settings data are processed through this service.
  • Google provides Google Sign-In when you choose that authentication method. Google's processing is also governed by Google's own privacy terms.
  • Google Fonts may be contacted to load the typefaces used by the website.
  • jsDelivr may be contacted to load the Supabase JavaScript client library used by the website.

These providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies. FACE ARENA does not control the independent privacy practices of these providers.

7. Sharing and disclosure

FACE ARENA does not sell personal information. Information may be visible to other users when that visibility is necessary for the competition, including display names, usernames, avatars, submitted competition photos, votes, comments, results, and public profile or history information according to the platform's features and settings.

Information is also processed by the service providers described above. It may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform, or the rights and safety of others.

8. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate FACE ARENA, maintain accounts, provide competition features, preserve battle results, and protect the integrity of rankings and historical records. Photos and competition records may therefore remain after an individual battle is complete.

You can request deletion directly from Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete account. The process removes the authentication account, profile, avatar, uploaded photo files, personal votes and comments, notifications, and push subscriptions. Active battles involving the account are cancelled; the other participant's photo is returned to the queue.

Completed battle outcomes and aggregate vote totals are retained in an anonymized form to preserve competition integrity and historical statistics. The deleted participant's profile, name, account association, and photo files are removed. An old result may remain as an anonymous result without its original image.

If the self-service deletion process fails, contact official Telegram support at @Facearenan. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

9. Security

FACE ARENA uses authentication and database access controls provided by its backend infrastructure. Stored competition photos are delivered using time-limited signed links where applicable. These measures are intended to reduce unauthorized access, but no internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

FACE ARENA is not intended for children under 13, and the platform does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to the platform, contact the platform operator using the contact details published by FACE ARENA so the matter can be reviewed.

11. International processing

Because FACE ARENA uses third-party infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. The location and safeguards applicable to processing by a provider are also governed by that provider's terms.

12. Your choices and privacy requests

You can use the privacy and notification settings available in FACE ARENA to manage certain profile, history, and notification preferences. You may also request information about personal data associated with your account or ask for correction or deletion where applicable.

For privacy-related requests, contact official FACE ARENA Telegram: @Facearenan. Never send passwords or authentication codes.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the platform's features, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

14. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact official FACE ARENA Telegram support: @Facearenan.
